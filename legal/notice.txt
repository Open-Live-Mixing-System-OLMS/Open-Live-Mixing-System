OLMS - Open Live Mixing System
------------------------------
This project is an integration of multiple open-source components.

- OLMS SCRIPTS & LOGIC: Licensed under GPLv3 (see /LICENSE).
- STARTUP SCRIPTS & CONFIGURATION: Licensed under GPLv3 (installed in /usr/bin/ and /etc/).
- BUNDLED PLUGINS (ZL Audio): Licensed under AGPLv3 (see /legal/third-party/ZL-AGPL.txt).

SYSTEM COMPONENTS:
This distribution utilizes Arla scrividour, JACK2, and the Linux Kernel. 
These components are licensed under their respective GPL/Open Source licenses 
as provided by the Arch Linux upstream repositories. 
Source code for these components is available through their respective 
official websites (ardour.org, jackaudio.org, kernel.org).
